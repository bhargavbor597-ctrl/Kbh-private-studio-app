Privacy Policy for Auto Clicker

Package ID: com.kbh.precisionautoclicker • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Summary: Auto Clicker operates 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, track, sell, or transmit personal data, screen content, keystrokes, or usage telemetry to external servers.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Auto Clicker (com.kbh.precisionautoclicker), a personal-purpose software testing, UI verification, and accessibility automation utility, handles device permissions and local configuration storage.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Auto Clicker is engineered with a strict zero-collection architecture. The application does not collect, access, store externally, or share:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, email address, phone number, or account credentials).
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, or precise geolocation data.
  • Keystrokes, passwords, notifications, financial details, or personal messages.
  • Screenshots, screen recordings, or analytics/crash telemetry sent to third-party servers.

3. Local On-Device Storage

All user-created automation profiles, target screen coordinates, timing intervals, hold durations, and interface preferences (such as dark/light theme and marker appearance) are saved exclusively on your local device using Android Room SQLite, SharedPreferences, or browser localStorage. You maintain full control and may delete saved presets inside the app or remove all stored data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the application.

4. Android AccessibilityService API Disclosure

To execute automated touch testing and repetitive click workflows on Android, Auto Clicker requests permission to use the Android AccessibilityService API. This permission is strictly limited to:

  • Dispatching touch gestures (taps and timed holds via dispatchGesture()) at the exact screen coordinates configured by the user.
  • Inspecting active window transition status strictly for real-click test diagnostics.

Auto Clicker never uses the AccessibilityService to collect personal data, monitor text input, bypass security prompts, or transmit screen contents off your device.

5. Display Over Other Apps (Overlay Permission)

The SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission is used solely to render floating control widgets (Start, Pause, Stop, Add Target) and draggable target markers over other applications during testing or automation sessions. You can toggle off the overlay at any time inside the app or revoke the permission in Android System Settings.

6. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Auto Clicker does not embed third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, or external data brokers. No user data is shared with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

This software testing utility is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
